\section{Conclusion}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Three Key Contributions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item[1.] \textbf{Network topology matters more than size:} Nestedness organization drives investment efficiency (not just community size or geographic distribution alone)
  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \item[2.] \textbf{Bridging ecology and finance:} Successfully applied ecological concepts (nestedness, bipartite networks) to understand venture capital syndication patterns (novel interdisciplinary approach)
  \vspace{0.5cm}
  \item[3.] \textbf{Geographic clustering enables hierarchy:} Silicon Valley's exceptional concentration facilitates nested organization
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}
\frametitle{Limitations \& Future Research}

\begin{block}{Limitations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Crunchbase data may under-represent certain sectors/regions
    \item Focus on U.S. companies limits international generalizability
    \item Cannot establish causality between network structure and outcomes
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\begin{block}{Future Research Directions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Causal analysis of network evolution mechanisms
    \item Cross-country comparison of investor network structures
    \item Connectance thresholds for nestedness emergence
    \item Dynamic modeling of network rewiring processes
    \item Investor-level nestedness contribution analysis
\end{itemize}
\end{block}

\end{frame}
